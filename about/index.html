About

Michaela Barnes

I am a writer of essays and short stories. I earned a B.S. in Television and Film Production from Syracuse University and worked as a Field Producer in Public Television in Boston and San Francisco. I was also an IACUC board member at Takeda Pharmaceuticals, and a substitute elementary school teacher. I love to photograph what moves me, sculpt the human figure, and find adventure.
